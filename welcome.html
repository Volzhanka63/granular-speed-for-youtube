Three ways to change speed

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Shift + . faster
  • Shift + , slower
  • R 1× and back to your speed

These are YouTube’s own shortcuts, so there’s nothing new to learn. They work with any keyboard language and stay out of the way while you type a comment or a search.

The speed button

Your speed shows in the player’s controls: bottom right on a video, top right on a Short. Scroll over it to change speed, or click it for a slider and presets. The settings icon in that panel opens all settings without leaving the video.

Hold to double

Hold the mouse on the video for twice your speed, up to 4×. At 1.5× you get 3×. Let go and you’re back.

Open YouTube and try it
How your speed stays set

YouTube resets the speed on every new video and ad. Granular Speed puts yours back each time, so it carries over to the next video, other tabs and the next time you open Firefox. YouTube’s own speed menu follows it too, so there’s only ever one speed to think about. Hover previews in the feed stay at normal speed. If you use another speed add-on, turn it off on YouTube, or the two will keep undoing each other.

Your steps and range

Out of the box, speed moves 0.25× at a time up to 2×, then 0.1×. Near the top of what you can follow, one small step decides whether you keep up with the speaker, so the steps get finer there. Everyone’s comfortable speed is different, so you can change both steps, where they switch, and your range in the settings.

Where it works
  • Regular videos on youtube.com, including theater mode, fullscreen and the miniplayer
  • Shorts
  • Live streams. At the live edge there’s nothing ahead to speed through, so the add-on leaves the speed to YouTube. Go back a bit and your speed applies again.
  • YouTube Music, the mobile site, Firefox for Android, and YouTube videos embedded on other sites

Firefox mutes the sound above 8×. To keep it, raise media.audio.playbackrate.muting_threshold in about:config.

No accounts, no analytics, no network requests. The add-on only runs on youtube.com, and your settings stay in your Firefox profile.
