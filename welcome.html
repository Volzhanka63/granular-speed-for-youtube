Controls

Keys

  • Shift + . faster
  • Shift + , slower
  • R 1× and back to your speed

These are YouTube’s own speed keys, now without the 2× limit. They work on any keyboard layout, and only when a video is on the page. While you type a comment or a search, they type as usual.

Hold the video for double speed

Hold the mouse button down on the video and it plays at twice your speed, up to your fastest setting (4×). At 1.5× you get 3×. Let go and you’re back. YouTube’s own hold gives a flat 2×, which is slower than what you’re likely watching at, so the extension replaces it.

The speed button

It shows your current speed in the player’s controls: bottom right on a regular video, top right on a Short. Scroll over it to step the speed. Click it for a slider and one-click presets. The tune icon in that panel opens all settings without leaving the video.

How it works

Two step sizes

Out of the box, speed moves 0.25× at a time up to 2×, then 0.1×. Near the top of what you can follow, a small change decides whether you keep up with the speaker, so the steps get smaller there. You choose both step sizes and where they change.

Your speed sticks

YouTube resets the speed on every new video and ad. The extension puts yours back each time, so it carries over to the next video, other tabs and the next time you open Firefox. Hover previews in the feed stay at normal speed.

One speed control

Because the extension keeps your speed, YouTube’s own speed menu can’t change it. Pick a speed there and a bubble shows the speed you’re really at. If you use another speed extension, turn it off for YouTube, or the two will undo each other.

Where it works

  • Regular videos on youtube.com, including theater mode, fullscreen and the miniplayer
  • Shorts
  • Live streams. At the live edge there’s nothing ahead to speed through, so the extension leaves the speed to YouTube. Rewind and your speed applies again.
  • YouTube Music, the mobile site, Firefox for Android, and YouTube videos embedded on other sites

Firefox mutes the sound above 8×. To keep it, raise media.audio.playbackrate.muting_threshold in about:config.

Open YouTube

No accounts, no analytics, no network requests. The extension only has access to youtube.com, and your speed and settings stay in your Firefox profile.
