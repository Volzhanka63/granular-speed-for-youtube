Keys on YouTube

  • Shift + . faster
  • Shift + , slower
  • R 1× and back to your speed

They work on any keyboard layout and stay quiet while you type a comment.

The speed button

It sits next to the subtitles button, at the bottom of a regular video and at the top of a Short. Scroll over it to step the speed. Click it for a slider and one-click presets.

Your own steps

Out of the box, speed moves 0.25× at a time up to 2×, then 0.1×. Change both step sizes, where they switch, the range and the presets with the tune icon in that panel, or the toolbar button.
